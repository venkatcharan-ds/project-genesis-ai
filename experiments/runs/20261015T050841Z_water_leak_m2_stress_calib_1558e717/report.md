## Problem

STRESS TEST of the key Milestone 2 assumption: the daily calibration window is leak-free.
Here leaks may start at any minute, including inside the calibration window, so a
recalibrated detector can absorb the leak into its baseline. Measures how much the
Milestone 2 corrections lose when their assumption is violated.

## Limitations

- Single-segment simulator with Gaussian noise; real networks have topology, sensor faults, missing data, and non-stationary demand.
- Labels are perfect; real leak labels are delayed and uncertain.
- Results must be re-established on real measurements before any deployment claim.
