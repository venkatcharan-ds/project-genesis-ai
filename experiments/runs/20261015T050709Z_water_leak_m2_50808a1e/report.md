## Problem

Reduce false alarms in water-leak detection without losing leaks. Milestone 1 showed that
meter calibration bias and meter transport delay cause most false alarms. Test whether
causal per-day delay correction, daily baseline recalibration and alarm persistence fix
this, using pre-registered hypotheses judged on a held-out test set.

## Limitations

- Single-segment simulator with Gaussian noise; real networks have topology, sensor faults, missing data, and non-stationary demand.
- Labels are perfect; real leak labels are delayed and uncertain.
- Results must be re-established on real measurements before any deployment claim.
