## Problem

SHARP STRESS TEST of the daily-recalibration assumption. Every leak is already
running at minute 0 (as if it started overnight), so it is fully inside the
calibration window. Prediction registered BEFORE running: corrected detectors will
absorb these leaks into their baseline and lose leak-event recall versus raw detectors.

## Limitations

- Single-segment simulator with Gaussian noise; real networks have topology, sensor faults, missing data, and non-stationary demand.
- Labels are perfect; real leak labels are delayed and uncertain.
- Results must be re-established on real measurements before any deployment claim.
