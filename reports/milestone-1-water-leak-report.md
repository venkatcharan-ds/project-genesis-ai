## Problem

Detect water leaks in an instrumented pipe segment (inlet flow, outlet flow,
pressure; 1 sample/minute) as early as possible while keeping false alarms
rare. Constraint: detectors must be causal (no future data).

## Failure analysis (test set, tuned parameters)

### mass_balance_threshold

- Event recall — burst: 18/18, gradual: 12/15
- Missed leaks (peak rate reached in episode, L/min): gradual 0.41, gradual 0.67, gradual 2.91
- Smallest detected leak (peak rate): 1.25 L/min
- Leak-free episodes with ≥1 false alarm: 4 (with demand spike: 0.18, without: 0.12)
- Leak episodes with a false alarm before the leak started: 9
- Hypotheses for next iteration (**untested**):
  - Gradual leaks are missed more often than bursts (recall 0.80 vs 1.00); a longer integration window or trend feature may help.
  - False alarms are more frequent on episodes with demand spikes (0.18 vs 0.12); lag-compensating the outlet meter may reduce them.
  - False-alarm episodes have larger meter bias (|bias| 0.87 vs 0.44 L/min); per-episode baseline recalibration may help.

## Limitations

- Single-segment simulator with Gaussian noise; real networks have topology, sensor faults, missing data, and non-stationary demand.
- Labels are perfect; real leak labels are delayed and uncertain.
- Results must be re-established on real measurements before any deployment claim.
